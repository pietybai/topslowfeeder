About Top Slow Feeder

Pet Slow Feeding Products Manufacturer & B2B Supplier — Shenzhen, China

Who We Are

Top Slow Feeder is the pet slow feeding products brand of Shenzhen Qidian Electronic Technology Co., Ltd., based in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China.

We specialize in pet slow feeding products — slow feeder mats, dog lick mats, slow feeder bowls and feeding accessories — sourced from established supply chain partners with full OEM/ODM capability.

Our clients include wholesale buyers, pet brand owners, and Amazon FBA sellers across Europe and North America. We handle everything from product development and sampling to bulk production and direct FBA shipment.
