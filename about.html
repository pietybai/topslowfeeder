About Top Slow Feeder

Pet Slow Feeding Products Manufacturer & B2B Supplier — Shenzhen, China

Who We Are

Top Slow Feeder is the pet slow feeding products brand of Shenzhen Qidian Electronic Technology Co., Ltd., based in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China.

We make our own range of pet slow feeding products — slow feeder mats, dog lick mats, slow feeder bowls and feeding accessories — at our plant in Guangdong, with full OEM/ODM capability.

Our clients include wholesale buyers, pet brand owners, and Amazon FBA sellers across Europe and North America. We handle everything from product development and sampling to bulk production and direct FBA shipment.

Certifications & Compliance

The silicone materials we use are supplied with LFGB and FDA test reports covering the tested material grades. Report coverage by SKU, colour and component is confirmed with your quotation, and full reports are available on request.

LFGB

German / EU food-contact safety standard for silicone materials. Required by most European retailers and pet brands.

FDA

US FDA food-contact compliance for silicone. Essential for selling pet feeding products in the North American market.

REACH SVHC

EU REACH Substances of Very High Concern compliance. Mandatory for all products entering the European market.

RoHS

Restriction of Hazardous Substances directive compliance. Verifies absence of lead, mercury, cadmium and other restricted materials.

California Prop 65

California Proposition 65 compliance. Critical for Amazon FBA sellers and retailers serving the US market.

SGS Biocompatibility

Third-party SGS biocompatibility testing confirms our silicone is safe for direct animal contact and food interaction.

TSCA

US Toxic Substances Control Act compliance for silicone raw materials. Covers 5-item chemical safety assessment.

PFAS-Free (675 Items)

Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances testing across 675 items. Meets the latest EU and US restrictions on PFAS in consumer products.

MSDS

English-language Material Safety Data Sheet for our silicone compound. Available for B2B compliance review and import documentation.

Need the full test reports for your compliance review? Contact us and we'll send them over.
