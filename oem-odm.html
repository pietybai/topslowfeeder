Frequently Asked Questions

What is your MOQ for OEM/ODM orders?

MOQ varies by product and customization scope. Please contact us with your requirements and we will confirm the MOQ for your specific product.

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We offer samples for evaluation before bulk production. Sample lead time is approximately 7 days. Contact us for sample pricing and shipping arrangements.

How long does bulk production take?

Standard bulk production is 15–25 days after sample approval and order confirmation. Lead time may vary during peak seasons.

Do you support Amazon FBA direct shipping?

Yes. We can handle FBA-compliant labeling, FNSKU barcodes, poly-bagging, and direct shipment to Amazon fulfillment centers. Please confirm FBA requirements with us at the order stage.

What customization options are available?

We support custom logo (print or emboss), brand colors, size adjustments, material variations, and full custom packaging including retail box, insert card, and label design.

What payment terms do you accept?

We accept bank T/T transfer and orders through Alibaba International. Payment terms are confirmed at order stage. Please contact us for details.
