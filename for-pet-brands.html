Frequently Asked Questions

Can you manufacture products exclusively for my brand?

Yes. For brands with sufficient ongoing volumes, we offer design exclusivity — your custom color combination or pattern will not be produced for any other buyer. Contact us to discuss the terms and volume thresholds for exclusivity.

What customization options are available?

We support custom logo (emboss or print), Pantone-matched brand colors, retail box and packaging design, insert cards, and ODM product development with new shapes or patterns. The full scope depends on your requirements — tell us what you need and we will confirm what is possible.

How long does a custom branded sample take?

For logo and color customization on existing product shapes, sample lead time is approximately 7 days. For ODM projects requiring new mold development, the timeline depends on design complexity. We will confirm the lead time after reviewing your brief.

Do you sign NDA agreements?

Yes. We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before discussing your product designs or brand concepts. This is standard practice for us when working with brand clients.

What is the MOQ for custom branded products?

MOQ depends on the level of customization. Simple logo placement on existing products has a lower MOQ than full ODM with new mold development. Contact us with your specific requirements and we will provide exact MOQ and pricing.

Can you handle packaging design and printing?

Yes. We can coordinate full retail packaging production at the factory — retail box design, insert cards, labels, and barcodes. You provide brand guidelines and artwork (or we can work with your designer), and we manage printing and assembly.
