Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for wholesale?

MOQ varies by product. We offer flexible starting quantities to help new wholesale partners test the market. Contact us and we will confirm the MOQ for your specific target SKUs.

Can I mix different products in one order?

Yes. We support mixed orders combining multiple SKUs. We can consolidate everything into a single shipment to keep freight costs efficient.

How quickly can you restock?

Standard restocking production is 15–25 days. For regular wholesale partners we maintain raw material inventory to shorten lead times. We recommend placing replenishment orders before your stock runs out.

Do you provide food safety certificates?

Yes. Food-contact material certificates and material safety documentation are available on request. These are suitable for EU and US import requirements. Please specify what documents you need when enquiring.

Can I add my own label to the products?

Yes. We can add your brand label or custom packaging for wholesale orders. This is a simple form of OEM that does not require a high MOQ. Ask us for details.

What shipping methods are available?

We ship by sea (LCL and FCL) and air freight. We can also ship via express courier for smaller orders or urgent restocking. We handle all export documentation.
