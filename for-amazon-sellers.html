Frequently Asked Questions

Can you ship directly to Amazon FBA warehouses?

Yes. We can ship directly to Amazon fulfillment centers in the US, UK, EU and other markets. We prepare FBA-compliant carton labels, shipping marks, and all export documentation. Just share your FBA shipment plan and warehouse address.

Can you apply FNSKU barcodes to the products?

Yes. Send us your FNSKU barcodes and we apply them to each unit during the packaging stage. We can place labels on the product itself, the poly bag, or the retail box — whichever Amazon requires for your listing.

What is the MOQ for Amazon sellers?

We offer low starting MOQs for FBA sellers testing a new product. The exact number depends on which product you choose. Contact us and we will confirm the minimum for your specific SKU.

Do your products comply with US and EU safety requirements?

Our products are made from food-contact grade silicone. We can provide material safety documentation and food-contact certificates on request. If you have specific compliance requirements (CPSC, REACH, etc.), please let us know and we will confirm what documentation is available.

Can I add my brand logo to the products?

Yes. We offer private label customization — your logo on the product and packaging. This is available from standard MOQs. For full custom design (new patterns, custom colors), see our OEM/ODM service.

How do I handle restocking when my inventory runs low?

Standard restocking production is 15–25 days. We recommend placing a restock order when you have 30–40 days of inventory remaining to account for production and shipping time. Regular buyers can discuss priority production slots with us.
