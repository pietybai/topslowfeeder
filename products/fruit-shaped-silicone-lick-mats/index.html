Product Details

Nine styles, one display

The range exists for two reasons: retail shelf appeal and mixed orders. A shop can hang a strawberry, two grapes and an ice cream cone together and get a colourful block, while a brand can take a single shape and colour and build a collection around it. All nine share the same silicone wall thickness and cup depth.

Where the food goes

Each shape has shallow cups or a raised grid rather than a flat surface: grapes are a cluster of round cups, the pizza slice has pepperoni-shaped wells, the donut has sprinkle ridges and a hole in the middle. Spreads and wet food sit in the cups; kibble does not work as well on these shapes, which is why we list them as lick mats rather than slow feeder mats.

Suction and wall mounting

The back carries suction cups, so the mat can be pressed onto a floor tile, a glass door or a tiled wall. Wall mounting is useful for a dog that licks standing up, or for keeping a cat's mat away from a food bowl. Smooth tile, glass and stainless steel hold best; textured vinyl, grout lines and wood do not.

Cleaning and storage

Warm soapy water or the top rack of a dishwasher. The cups are rounded, so food lifts out without a brush. Because the shapes are thin, nine mats stack flat in a drawer or a retail display.

Colours and OEM

Stock colourways are strawberry red, grapes in crystal purple, blush pink and green, donut pink, avocado green, pizza yellow and ice cream in blue or yellow. Custom Pantone colours, embossed logos and retail packaging are available for OEM orders. Sample lead time is about 7 days; production runs 15–25 days after sample approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many shapes are there?

Six shapes — strawberry, grapes, donut, avocado, pizza slice and ice cream cone — in nine stock colourways. Grapes come in three colours and the ice cream cone in two.

What size are they?

Between 150 × 210 mm and 210 × 210 mm depending on the shape, and about 120–190 g each. The grapes are the largest at 162 × 260 mm.

Do they stick to the wall?

Yes. The back has suction cups, so the mat can be mounted on a tiled wall or a glass door for standing licking, as well as used flat on the floor.

Is this a slow feeder or a lick mat?

A lick mat. The cups are designed for spreads, yoghurt, pâté and wet food; for dry kibble our mazes and bowl-style slow feeders work better.

What is the minimum order quantity?

From 10 pcs per style for stock colours. Mixed shape and colour orders are quoted together; custom colours, logo and packaging are quoted per order.
