Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a lick mat and a slow feeder mat?

A lick mat is designed for spreadable food — peanut butter, yogurt, wet food — and slows licking. A slow feeder mat uses deeper ridges or cells to slow down dry kibble. Several mats in this range work for both.

Are these mats suitable for cats?

Yes. The smaller formats suit cats and puppies; larger mats are aimed at medium and large dogs.
