Product Details

Two Products, One SKU

The LM-021 pairs a paw-shaped slow feeder mat with a 4-cavity frozen treat mold. The slow feeder mat features raised paw-print channels that guide dogs and cats to eat at a slower, safer pace. The frozen treat mold lets pet owners prepare homemade frozen treats — yogurt, broth, puree — in paw and bone shapes. Sold as a set or as individual components (mat only or mold only). Suitable for dogs and cats of all sizes.

Why the Set Format Works for Retail

Bundled sets consistently perform well on Amazon and in pet retail because they offer higher perceived value at a moderate price point. The slow feeder mat and frozen treat mold address complementary use cases — daily feeding and enrichment treats — which means the set appeals to a broader range of end consumers than either product alone.

Sizes Available

Two size options are available for both the mat and mold. The large mat (Ø175mm, approx. 167g) pairs with the large mold (approx. 126×126mm, 102g). The small mat (Ø160mm, approx. 106g) pairs with the small mold (approx. 104×104mm, 71g). Please confirm your size requirement when placing a sample or bulk order inquiry.

Material & Safety

Both mat and mold are made from food-contact safe silicone. Non-toxic, BPA-free and odorless. Suitable for direct food contact including frozen use. Certification documents available upon request.

Cleaning

Both mat and mold are dishwasher safe — top rack recommended. Can also be rinsed under running water. Silicone does not retain odors or stains from food.

OEM & Private Label

FAQ

Can I order the mat and mold separately?

Yes. Both the slow feeder mat and the frozen treat mold are available as individual SKUs. Contact us to confirm pricing and MOQ for individual components.

Is the mold safe for freezing?

Yes. The silicone mold is food-contact safe and suitable for freezer use. It can be used to freeze yogurt, broth, puree or similar foods into treat shapes.

Which size should I order?

Both sizes are suitable for dogs and cats of all breeds. The large set (mat Ø175mm) is more commonly ordered for mid-to-large dog breeds. If you are unsure, we recommend requesting samples of both sizes before placing a bulk order.

Do you ship directly to Amazon FBA warehouses?

Yes. We handle FNSKU labeling and FBA-compliant packaging and ship directly to Amazon fulfillment centers in the US, UK and EU.
