Product Details

The LM-021 set pairs a paw/claw-shaped silicone slow feeder mat with a 4-cavity frozen treat mold. The slow feeder mat features raised paw-print channels that guide dogs to eat at a slower pace. The included mold allows pet owners to freeze homemade treats such as yogurt, broth or puree.

Two mat sizes and two mold sizes are available — please confirm which size pairing applies to your order when inquiring. The set can also be sourced as individual components (mat only or mold only).

Available for OEM private label with custom logo, color and branded packaging. Material certifications and cleaning/usage specifications available upon request.
