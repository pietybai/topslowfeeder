Product Details

Two-in-One Feeding Solution

The LM-020 combines a slow feeder zone and a lick bowl zone within a single integrated tray. The slow feeder side features raised ridges that extend mealtime for dry food. The lick bowl side provides a smooth surface for wet food, broth, yogurt or peanut butter. The two-zone design gives pet brands and Amazon sellers a product that serves a wider range of feeding needs in one SKU — reducing the number of separate items a buyer needs to source.

Anti-Spill Tray Design

The integrated tray wall contains food and liquid within the feeding area, reducing mess on surrounding floor surfaces. This is a practical feature that end consumers consistently highlight in product reviews — and a strong retail selling point for pet brand owners positioning products in the premium segment.

Material & Safety

The feeding surfaces are made from food-contact safe silicone. Structural components use nylon and PC. All materials are non-toxic and BPA-free. Suitable for dogs and cats of all sizes. Certification documents available upon request prior to order placement.

Cleaning

Dishwasher safe — top rack recommended. The silicone feeding surfaces can also be rinsed under running water. The tray design makes it straightforward to clean without disassembly.

FAQ

What is the difference between the slow feeder zone and the lick bowl zone?

The slow feeder zone has raised ridges that slow down eating of dry food or kibble. The lick bowl zone is a smooth silicone surface designed for wet food, broths, peanut butter or yogurt — food the pet licks rather than eats from raised channels.

Is this product suitable for cats as well as dogs?

Yes. The product works for both dogs and cats. The tray format is particularly practical for cats who prefer defined feeding areas.
