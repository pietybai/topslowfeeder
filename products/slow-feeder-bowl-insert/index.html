Product Details

Four Petal Pockets

The insert is split into four deep petal-shaped pockets by raised divider walls. Kibble has to be nosed out of each pocket instead of being swallowed in one pass, which stretches a meal out without changing what the dog eats.

Drop-In Fit for Most Bowls

At 120 mm across and 45 mm high, the insert drops into standard bowls — plastic, stainless steel, glass or ceramic. It suits bowls with an inner diameter from roughly 125 mm, so a distributor can offer it as an accessory instead of shipping a whole new bowl.

Grips the Bowl Bottom

Small suction nubs on the underside press against the bowl floor and stop the insert sliding while the dog works around the dividers. It lifts out in one piece for refilling and washing.

Food-Grade Silicone and Cleaning

Molded from food-grade silicone tested to LFGB and FDA standards, BPA-free and odourless. It is dishwasher safe on the top rack and flexible enough that dried food releases without scrubbing.

Four Colours and OEM

Stock colours are grey, black, orange and purple. Custom Pantone colours, embossed or printed logos and retail packaging are available for OEM orders. Sample lead time is about 7 days; production runs 15–25 days after sample approval.

Insert or New Bowl?

An insert is the cheaper route for a customer who already owns a good bowl; a dedicated slow feeder bowl is the better route for someone buying their first one. We make both, so you can list whichever fits your range — see our comparison of inserts against dedicated bowls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will it fit my bowl?

It fits bowls with an inner diameter from about 125 mm — standard plastic, stainless steel, glass and ceramic bowls. Measure the inside of the bowl before ordering.

Does it stay in place?

Small suction nubs on the base grip the bowl floor. Press the insert down before adding food.

Is it for dogs or cats?

Both. The 120 mm size suits small to medium dogs and cats; for large dogs use it as a treat puzzle rather than a full meal.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Yes, top rack. It is also flexible enough to rinse clean by hand.

What is the minimum order quantity?

From 10 pcs for stock colours. Custom colours and logo work start higher — contact us with your target quantity and market for exact pricing.
