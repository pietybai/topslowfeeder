Product Details

Three Textures on One Mat

The mat divides into three lick zones: deep honeycomb cells, bone-shaped grooves and a field of raised ridges. Thick spreads such as peanut butter or wet food sit in the honeycomb and grooves, while thinner treats coat the ridges. Different textures make the dog work for each bite instead of licking the surface clean in seconds.

Suction Base Keeps It in Place

Suction cups on the underside grip smooth floors, tiles, glass and bathtub surfaces. That stops the mat sliding when an eager dog licks or paws at it, and lets you use it as a bath-time or grooming distraction.

Two Sizes for Different Dogs

Size S measures 15.5 × 15.5 × 0.8 cm and weighs about 80 g, which suits small dogs, puppies and cats. Size L measures 20 × 20 × 0.8 cm and weighs about 134 g for medium and large dogs. Both are flexible, so they roll up for storage and travel.

Material & Safety

Made from food-grade silicone tested to LFGB and FDA standards. The material is BPA-free, PVC-free and odorless, does not chip or crack, and is safe for direct food contact including frozen treats.

Cleaning

Rinse under a tap or place on the top rack of a dishwasher. Because the mat is flexible, the texture grooves release dried food easily — no hard-to-reach corners like rigid plastic mats.

OEM & Private Label

Six stock colors are available, including multi-color marble options. Custom Pantone colors, embossed or printed logos, individual OPP bags and printed color boxes are available for OEM orders. Sample lead time is about 7 days; production runs 15–25 days after sample approval.

For Wholesale Buyers

Size S packs 200 pieces per 48 × 32 × 32 cm carton (gross 17.3 kg); size L packs 100 pieces per 44 × 23 × 39 cm carton (gross 14.3 kg). The flat, lightweight format keeps freight and FBA prep costs low, and both sizes can be mixed in a single order.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sizes are available?

Size S: 15.5 × 15.5 × 0.8 cm, approx. 80 g. Size L: 20 × 20 × 0.8 cm, approx. 134 g. Both have suction cups on the back.

Is it dishwasher and freezer safe?

Yes. The mat can go on the top rack of a dishwasher and can be frozen with wet food, yogurt or broth for a longer-lasting treat.
