Product Details

How the texture works

The inside wall carries vertical rows of silicone beads, and the base adds clusters of small stars. Kibble slides between them instead of sitting in one heap, so an eager eater has to work each piece free instead of taking a mouthful. The same texture holds wet food and spreads in place.

Size and fit

The bowl measures 160 mm across and 80 mm deep, about 250 g. That is a cat-sized bowl that also suits small and medium dogs. For large dogs or multi-bowl setups, the deeper bowls in the range are the better fit.

Flexible silicone

The wall is soft enough to turn inside out. Leftover wet food, peanut butter or stuck kibble comes away in one movement, and the bowl goes straight back to shape.

Cleaning and storage

Warm soapy water or the top rack of a dishwasher. Because the outside is rounded with nothing protruding, empty bowls nest inside each other for storage and carton packing.

Colours and OEM

Stock colours are light blue, turquoise, lavender, pink and yellow. Custom Pantone colours, embossed or printed logos and retail packaging are available for OEM orders. Sample lead time is about 7 days; production runs 15–25 days after sample approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size is the bowl?

160 mm across and 80 mm deep, about 250 g. It suits cats and small to medium dogs.

How does it slow eating?

The bead rows and star clusters break the inside surface into small pockets, so kibble has to be nosed out piece by piece instead of being swallowed in one mouthful.

Is it for dry food or wet food?

Both. Kibble sits between the beads; wet food and spreads sit in the pockets and along the wall.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Yes, on the top rack. Warm soapy water works too, and the bowl can be turned inside out for a quick rinse.

What is the minimum order quantity?

From 10 pcs for stock colours. Custom colours, logo and packaging are quoted per order.
