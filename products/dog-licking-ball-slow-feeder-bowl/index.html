Product Details

Rolling Ball Design for Interactive Feeding

The DB-001 combines a licking bowl with a rolling ball element, turning mealtime into an interactive activity. Dogs must work around the ball to access their food, which naturally slows down eating and provides mental stimulation. This design is particularly effective for dogs prone to bloating, gulping or anxiety-driven fast eating.

Suction Base for Stability

The integrated suction base keeps the bowl firmly in place on smooth surfaces during feeding. This prevents the bowl from sliding or tipping over, even when larger dogs are actively pushing or pawing at it. The suction cup is built into the base and requires no additional accessories.

Two Sizes for Different Breeds

Available in Size S (package 15×15×5cm, 170g) for small to medium dogs and cats, and Size L (package 18×18×8cm, 335g) for medium to large breeds. Both sizes accommodate dry kibble and wet food. The size difference also makes it easy to offer tiered pricing or bundle options for retail.

Material & Safety

Made from food-contact safe silicone. Soft, flexible and non-toxic — safe for direct contact with pet food and saliva. Easy to clean by hand or in the dishwasher. Certification documents including LFGB and FDA are available upon request for compliance review.

OEM/ODM Customization

Available for custom logo printing, color matching (Pantone), and retail packaging design. Suitable for pet brands looking to private-label a lick bowl product or differentiate with a unique colorway. Sample lead time approximately 7 days; bulk production 15–25 days after sample approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the rolling ball removable for cleaning?

Yes, the ball can be separated from the bowl base for thorough cleaning. Both parts are dishwasher-safe.

Is this suitable for wet food as well as dry kibble?

Yes. The bowl design works with both wet food (pâté, raw food) and dry kibble. The suction base helps prevent spills when used with wetter textures.
